## New in 1.1.0
**Employers**
- Launch offer: the first 5 jobs per employer until 31 December 2026 get a SLE 0 invoice automatically; the dashboard shows how many free postings are left.
- Standard posting price (SLE 5,000 + GST) is filled in on new invoices automatically.
- Screening questions: up to 5 yes/no questions per job, with the answer wanted. The candidate table shows a match score and a "Meets all screening answers" filter.
- Bulk actions: select candidates, move them to a stage and send one message, from ready-made templates (shortlist, assessment, interview, offer, hired, not selected). Templates are also in the message box on each candidate.
- Team access: the company owner adds colleagues by email (they must have an account) and can remove them.
- Featured listings: "Feature this job" issues an invoice at the featured price (SLE 1,500 + GST by default); an admin switches featuring on once paid.
- Jobs with an external link show how many people tapped "Apply on website".

**Job seekers**
- "No PDF CV? Create one from my profile" on the Profile page and in the apply flow.
- Screening questions step when a job has them.
- Career tips page (Profile and sidebar); admins can replace the text in Content.

**Admins**
- Traction page: totals, month-by-month growth, reach and revenue, with "Copy summary" and spreadsheet download for the deck and plan.
- Scam warning flags in Job moderation (fees, WhatsApp-only contact, shortened links, unrealistic pay), with a "Flagged" filter; "Feature requests" filter.
- Platform settings: posting price, featured price, free postings and launch-offer end date.
- Invoices → Monthly report: revenue, GST, payments received and unpaid, with spreadsheet download.
- Referrals → Payouts: rewards due with Mobile Money numbers and total, spreadsheet download and "Mark all as paid" with a payment reference.
