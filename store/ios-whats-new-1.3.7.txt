For job seekers
• Get an alert for every new job as soon as it goes live, on your phone and in the app. Turn it on or off in Notification settings: "Alert me about every new job".

For employers
• New "Company info & how to pay" in the menu: our bank (Ecobank) and Orange Money details, Vocation SL company information, and our NRA and CAC certificates and bank letter, which you can view and download.
• Invoices show how to pay, with the invoice number to quote as your reference.
• A job stays open for up to 4 weeks. To keep it open longer, use Extend in My jobs.
