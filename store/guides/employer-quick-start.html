  1. Go to employers.vocationsl.com, or install Vocation SL from Google Play, and choose Hire talent.
  2. Tap Sign up, enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters), or use Continue with Google.
  3. Open the email we send and tap Confirm my email. It takes you straight to company setup.

  1. Go to My jobs and tap Post a job.
  2. Fill in the job title, location, industry, salary, summary, full description and closing date. Add screening questions or ask for documents if you need them.
  3. Tap Post job, or Save draft to finish later. Until your company is approved, jobs wait for a quick review before going live.

  1. Open Candidates. A red badge shows how many new applications are waiting.
  2. Tap an applicant to read their CV and documents, then move them through Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired.
  3. Message them, Offer interview times (they pick one and both get a reminder), and send an Offer letter PDF.

Company review

Our team checks every new company to keep listings genuine. You'll see the status change in the app once it's approved.

Find talent

Once approved, search job seekers who are open to work, view their profiles and invite them to apply.

Fees

Charges for job listings are agreed with each employer. Where a fee applies, we send an invoice after the listing is approved. Ask us for current rates.
