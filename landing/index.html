For job seekers

Everything you need to land the job

Designed for how people in Sierra Leone actually look for work: on a phone, sometimes on a weak connection, across every district.

Jobs in all 16 districts

Search by title, skill or company, and filter by district, industry, job type, experience, salary and remote work.

Apply in a few steps

Send your CV, a cover letter and a note to the employer. PDF, DOC and DOCX files are accepted.

Track every application

See each step, from submitted and viewed to shortlisted, interview and offer, with a clear timeline.

Talk to employers

When an employer messages you about an application, reply right inside the app. No lost phone numbers or missed calls.

Alerts, even when closed

Get notified about messages, interview invitations and status changes, on Android and in your browser.

Works on weak connections

Jobs and your applications are saved on your phone. Keep browsing offline; changes sync when you're back online.

Follow your progress, get alerts and reply to employers until you get the offer.

Trust & safety

Genuine jobs, protected data

Every job is reviewed

Companies and job listings are checked by our team before they are published. Verified companies carry a check mark.

Your CV stays private

Your documents are shared only with the employers you apply to. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Report anything suspicious

Any listing can be reported in one tap. Our team reviews every report and removes listings that break the rules.

Never pay to get a job. A genuine employer will never ask you for money to apply, to attend an interview, or for a uniform, training or medical before you are hired. If anyone does, report the job.

Making opportunity easier to reach

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Vocation SL free?

Yes, for job seekers. Searching, saving jobs, applying and messaging employers are all free. Charges for employer job listings are agreed with each employer.

Which parts of Sierra Leone are covered?

All 16 districts, from Western Area Urban and Rural to Kailahun, Kono, Falaba, Karene and Pujehun, plus main towns like Makeni, Koidu and Lunsar.

Do I need a good internet connection?

No. The app saves jobs and your applications on your phone, so you can keep browsing offline. Anything you change is sent when you reconnect.

Why isn't the app on Google Play yet?

We're preparing the store listing. The Android app on this page is the same app, offered as a direct download in the meantime.

How do employers join?

Sign in and choose Hire talent, then register your company. New companies and every job are reviewed by our team before going live.

How do I get help?

Email us at vocationxsl@gmail.com.
