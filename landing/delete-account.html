Delete your Vocation SL account

How to permanently delete your account and data.

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

In the Android app: job seekers open Profile → Settings → Delete account. Employers open More → Delete account.

On the web: sign in at app.vocationsl.com, then go to Settings → Delete account (employers: the menu → Delete account).

Confirm, and your account is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email vocationxsl@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and delete it.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details, your profile (photo, experience, education, skills, preferences), your CVs and cover letters, your saved jobs and searches, your applications and messages, your alerts and the devices registered for push notifications. If you are the only member of an employer company, the company profile and its job listings are deleted too.

What we keep

Records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons, such as invoices issued to employers and our record of actions taken by our admin team, may be kept for as long as required. They are not used for any other purpose.
